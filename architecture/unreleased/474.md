- [vecs-protocol] Added: Per-world `CidLayout` and `NetworkWorld.cid`, allowing different network schemas in one process without changing wire bytes.
- [vecs-protocol] Removed: Exports `setLocalComponentMin`, `getLocalComponentMin`, `LOCAL_COMPONENT_MIN`, `cid_pack` and `cid_unpack`; use the owning world's `cid.pack` / `cid.unpack` instead.
- [vecs-protocol] Changed: `ComponentSnapshot` and `StateDiff` constructors require a layout; decode them and `Server2Client` with `decodeWith(layout, decoder)`.
- [vecs-client] Changed: `DiffBuffer`, `Diff` and `ComponentSnapshot` constructors take the layout first.
- [vecs-server] Tests: Cover concurrent multi-schema replication; protocol tests cover invalid layout construction and sector-schema overflow bounds.
