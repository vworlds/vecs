- [vecs] Added: Declare `world.component(X).add(Requires, world.component(Y))`, the owning counterpart of `With`: adding `X` adds `Y`, and removing the last bringer of `Y` from a live entity removes `Y`. `ComponentMeta.bringers` and `isBroughtOn(entity)` expose the `With`/`Requires` bringers (#463).
- [vecs-client] Changed: Network removals of components that `With` or `Requires` can bring are settled once the diff has applied, by a new `ClientWorld:Release` system after `ClientWorld:Apply` (or at the end of `apply()`), replacing the per-diff removal index. A chain of network bringers removed together now goes as a whole (#463).
- [vecs-phaser-server] Changed: `DeadReckoned` owns `Motion` and its prediction through `Requires` and keeps `With` for the physics `Rotation`; its `remove` hook no longer removes them (#463).
