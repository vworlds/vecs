- [repo]: Added `bench/core-storage.ts`, the storage epic's benchmark cases at 10,000 and 100,000 entities: iteration without injection, injected in-place writes, deferred `set`, watcher delivery, component churn and retained heap per entity in a fresh process. Each case prints a `CORE_STORAGE_RESULT` line with median, min and max nanoseconds per entity per tick over fixed tick counts and a checksum of component field sums and callback counts that is identical across builds, and `bench:release-check` compares every case with the checksums as fingerprints.
