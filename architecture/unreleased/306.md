- [vecs] [vecs-client] Changed, **breaking**: calling `dispose()` inside a tick now throws, as do calls inside `defer`, a command drain or an entity's destroy (#305, #306). Refusal happens before teardown starts; dispose synchronously at top level after the enclosing operation finishes. Ordinary synchronous top-level mutation hooks remain allowed, and a second completed top-level disposal stays a no-op. Remove the core frame/drain/cascade disposal boundaries while retaining infrastructure ordering, singleton/trait cleanup and named survivor errors. ClientWorld holds every disconnect raised during `progress()` / `apply()` until the outermost call finishes; its handlers may dispose, while socket closure and RPC rejection remain immediate.
