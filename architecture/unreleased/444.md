- [vecs] Added: Cleanup policies are pair-form traits. `OnDelete` takes `Throw` (default), `Remove` or `Delete` and governs a component entity's carriers; `OnDeleteTarget` takes `Remove` (default) or `Delete` and governs a relationship's sources. Declare one with `world.component(R).add(OnDeleteTarget, Delete)`; trait reflection and `vecs_components` now report it (#444).
- [vecs] Removed: The public `ComponentMeta.onDelete` and `ComponentMeta.onDeleteTarget` fields and the `CleanupPolicy` export. Replace `meta.onDelete = CleanupPolicy.X` with `.add(OnDelete, X)` and `meta.onDeleteTarget = CleanupPolicy.X` with `.add(OnDeleteTarget, X)` (#444).
- [vecs-physics] Changed: `JointsModule` declares `JointBodyA` and `JointBodyB` cleanup with `(OnDeleteTarget, Delete)`; behaviour is unchanged (#444).
- [vecs-phaser-client] Changed: `DisplaySetupModule` declares `DrawIn` and `DrawBy` cleanup with `(OnDeleteTarget, Remove)`; behaviour is unchanged (#444).
