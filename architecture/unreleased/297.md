- [vecs] [vecs-client]: `World.dispose()` now destroys infrastructure and singleton data after application cleanup (#296, #297), so module-owned resources can release through `remove` hooks. The subsequent #305/#306 change above replaces its mid-tick, deferred and destroy-hook completion boundaries with synchronous top-level disposal. Component keys follow their carriers; if ordering stalls, an error names all registered survivors and their carriers instead of silently retaining them. Cycle breaking is deferred. `clearAllEntities()` retains its throwing behavior. This replaces the infrastructure-survives contract from #230; callers must discard the disposed world.
